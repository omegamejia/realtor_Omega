# Brand Voice, The Mashore Group

Produced by `content-engine-builder`. Read by `copywriter`, `hooks`,

**Source:** harvested from The Mashore Group's published marketing, confirmed in
the setup interview. Pulled 2026-07-30.

## 1. Ideal Dream Client

- **Who they are:** A Brentwood homeowner facing a move they did not entirely
  choose. Divorce, a death in the family, retirement, a job that moved, kids gone.
  Often two people who have to agree, usually on someone else's deadline.
- **What they are trying to do:** Move without being taken advantage of at the
  moment they have the least energy to fight about it.
- **What is in the way:** They do not know what the house is worth, they do not
  know if now is right, and they are afraid of a permanent decision made while
  everything else is unstable.

## 2. Voice of Customer, their exact words

- **Pain:** "overwhelmed" · "burdened by everything involved" · "we needed to sell
  his house" · "I don't know what it's worth" · "is it really the right time"
- **Desire:** "sell it fast" · "top dollar" · "make it enjoyable" · "positioned us
  so well" · "spot on"
- **Objection:** "should I take the first offer" · "is Zillow accurate" · "what do
  I have to disclose" · "we met with three different agents"

## 3. Authority stand

- **The stand:** Your agent is a mediator, not a salesperson. When the decision is
  emotional, the job is keeping it from becoming an expensive one.
- **What it means:** The advice does not change based on what closes fastest. She
  will tell you not to sell, not to renovate, or not to take the offer in front of
  you, even when that costs her the transaction.

## 4. Voice DNA

- **Sounds like:** Direct second person. Short sentences. Plain words. Warm about
  the situation, blunt about the numbers. Willing to say no outright.
- **Never sounds like:** Corporate, hedging, or luxury-brochure.
- **Banned:** em-dashes · "serves as" · "plays a vital role" · "when it comes to" ·
  "in today's market" · "elevate" · "unlock" · "nestled" · stacked exclamation marks
- **Rhythm:** Short declaratives, then one longer explaining sentence. Contractions
  on. Open a section with a direct question when it fits.

## 7. Hook menu

- Name the hard situation directly, then promise control
- Correct a belief the reader already holds ("Is Zillow accurate?")
- Refuse a common move ("Should you take the first offer? No.")
- Reframe the role ("Think of your agent as a mediator.")
- The question they are already asking at 11pm
