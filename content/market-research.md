## Why this file exists

A real build put "We will tell you when it is not worth much" as the headline of
an antique shop. True, distinctive, and written from instinct rather than from
any knowledge of what people actually want from an antique shop, which is
something interesting to buy or a house cleared. It was the largest type on the
page, telling the majority of visitors their possessions were probably worthless.

Nothing caught it, because nothing required anybody to find out first. The cost
of guessing is not a bad sentence. It is a whole site aimed at the wrong person.

**Never invent a figure here.** Every number carries its source. An unsourced
statistic is a fabrication, and this file feeds the copy that ships under a real
business's name. See `.claude/rules/truth-verification.md`.
